Connect your own ElevenLabs account for studio-quality voices and MP3 download.

Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and is sent only to api.elevenlabs.io. Never share your key or paste it on a computer you do not own. Audio made with the API uses your ElevenLabs credits.

Free browser voices

These use the voices installed on your phone or computer. Quality and language support (for example Urdu) depend on your device. They cannot be saved as an MP3 file. For MP3 files, use the ElevenLabs API mode.
